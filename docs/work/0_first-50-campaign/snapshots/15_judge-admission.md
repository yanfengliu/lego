It also derives all compatible top/base pairs among the validated reported edges for every retained finite camera row and requires set equality. Group signs and the four-group aggregate are derived from these validated records before comparison to the estimator's reported booleans.

The unchanged three-pixel allowance is the sum of the two declared 1.5-pixel edge uncertainties. It remains a conservative pixel-class compatibility allowance; it does not assign exact continuous locations to pixels. Exact midpoint and interval endpoint equality uses half-integer arithmetic.
