Concrete falsifiers are:

1. Two admissible signed-axis interpretations survive the required observations or controls, but the producer emits a unique signed authority anyway.
2. Four collinear top cores or identity order are treated as independent transverse-axis evidence, or a claimed base correspondence belongs to unbound predecessor geometry.
3. A required mirror case violates the corresponding axis sign law, or erasing the evidence still produces signed authority.
4. The expected branch comes from a favorable per-panel geometry result, or any Step-43 observation changes the frozen candidate or orientation law.
5. A required branch, registration classification, local containment or confirmation is omitted; an unsettled seed or incomplete runner population is treated as comparable evidence.
6. Any unchanged calibration threshold, mask identity, source extraction rule, denominator, genuine producer/consumer binding or native visual condition fails.
7. A full-rank-looking number or public receipt object bypasses the current unavailable producer, registered refusal or opaque brand.
8. A rejected global-search route is taken as a proof that every compliant source-camera method is impossible without demonstrating which preserved user requirement no such method can meet.
