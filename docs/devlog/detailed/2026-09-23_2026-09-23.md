# 2026-09-23

## `npm run booklet`: the first milestone of the booklet harness

**Action:** Added `tools/booklet`, a Node-only harness that reads the booklet PDF (pdf.js), aligns every official brick of set 21066 to a printed step, measures catalog coverage, checks the official LDraw export's frames, and replays the official model step by step through the brick kernel. It writes per-step rows to the ignored `output/booklet/status.json` and `output/booklet/reference-playback.json` (and refuses a `BOOKLET_OUT` that Git would not ignore), prints at most 40 summary lines, and compares headline counts with the committed `status/booklet-baseline.json`. The official model loads only through `tools/booklet/answer-key/`; an `eslint.config.js` rule bars product code (`packages/*/src`, `apps/*/src` and their config roots, every script extension) from loading `tools/booklet` or naming `output/official-model` as a path, and `tools/booklet/answer-key-guard.test.ts` lints each construct through the real config.

**Measured result:** A full run takes about 7 to 10 s. Read: steps 1..359 contiguous on pages 11-219 after removing 219 overprinted labels; 859 step-owned callouts sum to 1,464, and the only other callout-size labels are page 84's 4 labels / 14 pieces (an announcement) and page 132's 1 label / 1 piece on the bag-7 opening, 864 / 1,479 in all; the inventory has 276 rows / 1,465 pieces. Align: 338 steps match by run order, 18 are fitted by the local repair, and 3 (156-158) stay unmatched because the repair window 155-159 stopped at its 400,000-node budget; 1,464 bricks are assigned and the one brick no LXFML step adds is 31510. Catalog: 172 designs, 77 exact, 5 interchangeable mould revisions, 90 missing; the first uncovered design is needed at step 51 (86996) and the first absent colour at step 18 (LDraw 47). Pairing: 1,422 bricks are verified by instance agreement, and 42 designs placed once plus 1 multi-part brick are unverified. Export frames: of 8 pinned designs, 6 agree, 93273 differs from its pin only by the part's half-turn symmetry, and 77844 disagrees; 163 designs have no pin. Playback: valid through step 1 as exported; with the 77844 pin and the reviewed 80015 origin applied as a separate correction layer, valid through step 28, first failure at step 29 (`DISCONNECTED_ASSEMBLY`, 15573).

**Believed and proved false:** The older 881 / 1,512 source count was a different reading of the same pages. It is the 859 / 1,464 step callouts plus the 22 larger "2x"/"4x" sub-build multipliers (sum 48), which are not parts; the harness recomputes this every run. The committed "valid through step 28, blocked at 29" frontier was a placement search (first locally complete order per step), not a compile of official poses; the corrected official poses now reach the same step 28 on their own.

**What the review caught (review-g3a):** The first write-up said 359 of 359 steps aligned. 16 of them matched only because the repair chose their bricks to fit the callouts, and the window 155-159 was reported solved after its search hit the node budget, so its fit was never proven minimal. The summary now splits run order from repair and reports the budget window as unsolved. It also claimed the pairing was proved, but the invariance failures and colour conflicts were never read, and a design placed once cannot be checked by instance agreement at all. The alignment's doc claimed the inventory check "closes the gap"; it compares per-element totals and never sees how bricks are split into steps.

**The 80015 explanation, corrected:** The first write-up said turning 80015 a quarter turn clears the step-2 overlap, so the export's 80015 frame was the first suspect. The review showed the harness is not the cause: the registry frame for 80015.dat matches the catalog and the studs in 80015.dat. The export's 80015 frame is wrong in its origin, not its rotation. The quarter turn clears step 2 but disconnects step 1, while keeping the export's rotation and moving the origin to the stud at LDraw (-10, 8, -70) gives valid through step 25. 77844's export frame (origin (20, 8, 20)) contradicts `scripts/builder_ldraw_frame_pins.py` (turn180, (40, 8, 0)). Fixing both gives valid through step 28, first failure at step 29: the committed frontier.

**Trip hazards:** The 80015 correction is fitted by a playback search over the ring's stud positions, not derived from a Builder record; it applies only while the export shows exactly the reviewed frame, and the run checks each run that the new origin sits under a catalog stud. The frame registry (`output/real-build/history/prefix50-ldraw-catalog-frames-reviewed-move-bcf97021….json`) is ignored, so a clean clone has none: without it playback infers frames, and the inference disagrees with the registry on 24 of 35 parametric rows, which the run now prints in capitals. The harness used to drop the top-face offset for a parametric part whose catalog declares a turn, so the 2 x 14 plate (91988) landed 4 LDU off; `tools/booklet/frames.test.ts` pins the fix.
