# 2026-09-23

## `npm run booklet`: the first milestone of the booklet harness

**Action:** Added `tools/booklet`, a Node-only harness that reads the booklet PDF (pdf.js), aligns every official brick of set 21066 to a printed step, measures catalog coverage, and replays the official model step by step through the brick kernel. It writes per-step rows to the ignored `output/booklet/status.json` and `output/booklet/reference-playback.json`, prints at most 40 summary lines, and compares headline counts with the committed `status/booklet-baseline.json`. The official model loads only through `tools/booklet/answer-key/`; an `eslint.config.js` rule bars product code (`packages/*/src`, `apps/*/src`) from importing `tools/booklet` or naming `output/official-model`, and `tools/booklet/answer-key-guard.test.ts` lints violations through the real config.

**Measured result:** A full run takes about 14 s. Read: steps 1..359 contiguous on pages 11-219 after removing 219 overprinted labels; 859 step-owned callouts sum to 1,464, and the only other callout-size labels are page 84's 4 labels / 14 pieces (an announcement) and page 132's 1 label / 1 piece on the bag-7 opening, 864 / 1,479 in all; the inventory has 276 rows / 1,465 pieces. Align: 359 of 359 steps match (343 by run order, 7 repair windows moved 38 bricks); 1,464 bricks are assigned and the one brick no LXFML step adds is 31510. Catalog: 172 designs, 77 exact, 5 interchangeable mould revisions, 90 missing; the first uncovered design is needed at step 51 (86996) and the first absent colour at step 18 (LDraw 47). Playback: valid through step 1 only.

**Believed and proved false:** The older 881 / 1,512 source count was a different reading of the same pages. It is the 859 / 1,464 step callouts plus the 22 larger "2x"/"4x" sub-build multipliers (sum 48), which are not parts; the harness recomputes this every run. The committed "valid through step 28, blocked at 29" frontier was a placement search (first locally complete order per step), not a compile of official poses, so it is not the number this playback measures.

**Trip hazards:** The kernel's LDraw interchange gives a parametric catalog part a zero LDraw-to-catalog frame, but a parametric 2x4 plate is `upright-yaw-90` with `[0,-4,0]` (the measured first-50 frame registry). Playback therefore takes frames from that registry when it is present (610 of 629 placed parts), then from catalog declarations, then from a labelled top-of-body inference. The first invalid step is 2: 80015 (5x5 quarter ring) overlaps 30503 at the official export's pose, and 80015 collides again with 54383 at step 7. Turning 80015 a quarter turn clears the step-2 overlap, so the export's 80015 frame is the first suspect, not the catalog.
