## An open lookahead contour makes the panel a superset, not a non-answer

**Timestamp:** 2026-08-08 18:55

**Action:** Replaced the refusal with a change of measure. `PrefixAgreementInput` now carries `measure: "iou" | "containment"`, and `agreementAt` drops the `there && !here` arm of the union under containment. The deferred step selects it from whether the lookahead's highlight encloses any filled region.

**Reasoning:** This is printed step 5's correction one level up, and the previous session's own sentence applies unchanged: a refusal can be arithmetically correct about a question the panel does not answer. Measured over twelve sampled pages only 19 of 36 contours close, so a lookahead that can only read a closed one cannot finish this booklet. What the open case changes is not whether panel N+1 is evidence but what the evidence says: with nothing to subtract, `builtMask` is what step N built **plus** the pieces step N+1 places, so it is a superset of anything a step-N candidate can draw. Equality is then the wrong question and containment is the right one — and the same falsifiability survives, because a candidate spilling outside the panel's built art still loses. The existing `minimumDeferredAgreementMargin` and `minimumDeferredAgreement` are untouched.

## Four lattice rankings, and the one the session was handed is not the fix

**Timestamp:** 2026-08-08 18:00–18:55

**Action:** `canonicalPair` reads argument order as information, which is real: peaks arrive in score order, and a pair whose left-pointing member arrives first is dropped. Made it order-free and measured the joint effect the previous note asked for, over nineteen sampled panels dumped to `output/zzz-lattice`.

**Result, in the order they were tried and refuted:**

1. **Order-free pairing alone.** Recovers printed step 7's own grid at 0.21% of its pitch, az 55.2 / el 34.9 against neighbours at 54.2–55.1 / 34.5–35.2. It also admits printed step 4's `a=(14,6) b=(-25,21)` — a lattice on the fifth-of-a-pitch repeat that panel draws along one grid direction — which counts **7 of 16 peaks against the panel's own grid's 6** while sitting **23.75% of pitch** from any axonometric projection against 0.47%. Printed step 4 goes from fitted to refused. The 40-panel harness fails on exactly that assertion.
2. **Printability first.** Fixes both panels and removes the fit's ability to refuse, because it pre-selects a candidate the final residual gate is guaranteed to accept. All forty sampled panels then "fitted", including ones reporting **10.6, 21.0 and 77.2 points per stud** against the booklet's 16.1, and the harness's refused-panel medians became `NaN`.
3. **Harmonic response.** The module's own whole-picture measure, which `refineBasis` already maximises. Printed step 4's own grid is **second on this key too**, beaten by its own **index-4 superlattice `a=(72,29) b=(-50,41)` at exactly twice its pitch**, 0.1622 against 0.1401. So peaks-then-harmonics keeps the superlattice out and lets the fine lattice in, and harmonics-then-peaks does the reverse. Over the seventeen panels with a known camera the best lexicographic order of {peaks, printable, harmonic, coarsest, residual} scores 16 of 17, and the one it misses is printed step 4.
4. **`coherence` first** — the autocorrelation strength of the two repeats the pair is built from, already stored on every candidate and never consulted. It looked like the answer: **17 of 17** on the sampled printed panels, printed step 4 kept, printed step 7 recovered, fitted count 33 → 34 of 40, neighbour basis gap 0.176 → 0.148pt, and printed steps 11–15 and 36 converging on one basis at 0.10% of pitch where they used to report three between 0.10% and 1.56%. It puts a **synthetically drawn** grid's azimuth at **79.0 degrees where the test drew it at 34.0**, and takes eleven of this module's twenty tests with it.

**Reasoning:** Ground truth by construction beats agreement with neighbours. The nineteen-panel screen judged a camera "correct" by whether it matched what the panels either side measured, which is consensus; `camera-fit-lattice.test.ts` draws a grid at a known azimuth and asks for it back. A ranking a controlled grid falsifies is not a ranking, whatever it scores on pictures whose answer is only known by agreement. All four were reverted and the numbers written into `canonicalPair`'s and the comparator's own comments, so the next session starts from the measurement instead of the hypothesis.

**What is left:** the missing thing is a per-candidate measurement that separates a stud grid from a repeat that is not one, and none of `explainedPeaks`, `cellAreaPx`, `residualPx`, `harmonicScore` or `coherence` is it. The panel harness already computes something that looks like one — `foldedStudShape`'s radial contrast, 1.51 on accepted panels against 1.33 on refused — and computes it once for the chosen basis rather than per candidate. Whatever is tried next has to clear the synthetic grids first.

## One correctness fix survived, and it is measured

**Timestamp:** 2026-08-08 18:12

**Action:** `countExplained` no longer counts a peak as explained when the nearest lattice site is the candidate's own origin.

**Result:** Across the 40-panel harness exactly two panels change and both stay refused: printed step 7's refusal moves from **1.42px to 15.19px** from the closest axonometric projection, and printed step 32's from 2.48px to 2.65px. Every fitted panel is identical, the fitted count stays 33/40 and the camera-run segmentation stays 1-3, 4, 5-9, 10-15, 16-34, 36-37. The refused-panel separation improves: coherence 0.230 against 0.048 refused, where it was 0.230 against 0.136.

**Reasoning:** Every lattice has a site at (0,0) and the autocorrelation peak it is matched against is at a nonzero offset, so a peak "explained" by the origin is a repeat the lattice does not have. The tolerance is a fraction of the cell, so a coarse candidate's catchment around its origin is physically large and it collects short repeats wholesale: panel 7 had a 2448px-cell candidate claiming the peaks at (-8,3) and (-13,5), **8.5px and 13.9px away** on a picture whose grid step is 40px, and printed step 4 had a 4402px-cell candidate claiming (8,3) at 8.5px. Panel 7's old 1.42px refusal was misleading precisely because of it — it read as a panel that nearly fitted.
