## Three separate frauds had to be refused to read an honest stud pitch

**Timestamp:** 2026-07-31

**Action:** The estimator was rebuilt. Each of three failure modes was found by rendering the booklet and looking at it. (`3d61516`)

- A silhouette turns corners and one straight-line fit cannot flatten a piecewise-linear profile. Corners are now found by a two-piece least-squares split and only straight pieces measured.
- **Repeating proves nothing.** Rounding a sloped line into whole raster rows makes a staircase, and a staircase repeats exactly and forever — `round(0.45x)` has period 20. The edge must also move further than rounding can move it.
- **Moving far enough proves nothing either.** Thresholding an anti-aliased stroke makes the traced edge wander a row or more, correlated along its length; on page 120 that produced a confident **26 px** pitch from a visibly straight tiled outline. Only requiring the wobble to gather onto one period's harmonics, and to leave the half-multiples between them empty, refuses it.

**Code reviewer comments:** The estimator was chosen as an evaluator rather than authored: four independent approaches were built against a fixed benchmark and scored. **Two rivals reached the same synthetic ceiling while inventing pitches for smooth booklet art** — which is exactly what the real fixture exists to catch.

**Result:** On twelve edges traced from the sample and labelled by rendering each region at two scales and looking: **6/6 scalloped edges measured, 6/6 smooth edges refused, no false positives.** The scale check: rendering half again as large gives a pitch half again as big — observed **1.504, 1.525, 1.525 against an expected 1.5.** On a synthetic set of 150 measurable and 59 unmeasurable profiles, **recall 1.00 within 2%, no false positives.**

**Reasoning:** This is the first measurement about a step stated in brick units rather than pixels.

**Validation:** Across a twelve-page spread only **4 of 50 highlight regions** carry a measurable pitch. That is the honest yield, not a defect: most outlines are tiles, parts seen edge-on, or bricks whose studs sit inside the silhouette, and every refusal names what it measured and what would have passed.
