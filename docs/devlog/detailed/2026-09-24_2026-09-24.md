## G3c-2: alignment by identity, and identification scored against the official model

**Timestamp:** 2026-09-24, on d84979c

**Action:** `npm run booklet` gained an identify stage (`tools/booklet/identify-stage.ts`). It runs the product's closed-set identifier, `identifyBooklet`, on the booklet PDF alone: no model call, no answer key. It turns each printed step's callouts into an identity target: the pieces of each element identification trusts, plus the quantities of the callouts it flags (its residuals), which are matched by count only. The aligner (`align.ts`, `align-repair.ts`, criterion in `align-identity.ts`) holds each step to that target. The repair treats trusted callouts as fixed slots. When the smallest balanced window cannot be redistributed, a cluster held to identity tries larger balanced windows. The alignment by counts alone still runs as a comparison line. `identify-score.ts` scores identification against the model per step: agreement with the model's run order, the parts the booklet moves to another step (with direction), and each callout after alignment. The report lines are in `summary-identify.ts`. New limit tests cover `maxOperatorsPerPage` (`pdf-scan.test.ts`) and `maxDecodedPixelsPerPage` (`identify-page-pixels.test.ts`, which lowers the limit through a module mock).

**Result:** 359 steps: 316 exact identity (303 by run order, 13 re-laid by repair, 22 of the 316 print no callouts), 43 count fallback (65 flagged callouts), 0 mismatch; 9 repair windows, all solved, 44 bricks moved. Identity gives 22 steps other parts than counts did: 31-33, 52-54, 62, 63, 96, 98-100, 156-158, 227-229, 231, 232, 329 and 330. Identification against the model: 313 of 333 steps with callouts agree by run order. The other 20 differ only where the booklet prints a part in another step than the model builds it: 20 pieces earlier, 24 later, 0 unexplained. After alignment, 859 of 859 callouts agree. The 794 trusted callouts agree by construction; the 65 flagged ones are informative.
