# Local rules

- Use independent subagents for parallel repository work and put every material implementation unit through a separate harsh-critic pass before accepting it; when progress stalls, hold the reproduction or benchmark fixed, solicit deliberately different approaches, score them against the same evidence, and keep the best measured result rather than iterating one idea indefinitely.
- Treat user-provided and publicly reachable construction sources as authorized inputs for this repository's private, noncommercial reconstruction and evaluation work; commercial-rights review must not block that work. Keep native source payloads local when practical, preserve exact identities and hashes for reproducibility, and do not describe upstream material as project-owned or silently broaden the authorization to public redistribution or training.
- Bundle real LDraw geometry as the render layer, preserving per-file authorship and licence, and flip the BOM and third-party notices to "geometry bundled under CC BY 4.0, attribution required" at the first admission that actually bundles a file. Reuse is not training: that right stays unheld and separately recorded. Generated approximations were rejected because every built step is scored against its printed booklet panel, so lost curve fidelity fails the measurement the goal is judged by. (Owner decision, 2026-08-04.)
- Advance catalog, collision and validator truth versions at the first production part admission rather than when the contract changes, keeping the preceding version as a historical migration snapshot so existing documents keep hashing as they did. (Owner decision, 2026-08-04.)
- Cropped regions of the user's own instruction booklets — callout thumbnails and step panels — may be sent to a model for part identification and step classification. Crops only: never a whole booklet, never other repository content, never credentials, and nothing retained beyond local evidence under the ignored output roots. This is the separate consent the local-by-default rule requires, and it is scoped to booklet crops; it does not authorize sending any other user reference, design, or artifact. Model output remains untrusted data that proposes while a deterministic check disposes, and it can never self-certify or admit a part. (Owner decision, 2026-08-05, taken because the cues that distinguish a rotation from an attachment are inside the page raster and no deterministic reader can see them.)
- A part whose source frame is not centred carries an explicit source-to-catalog frame in its declaration, the way `80015` already carries `connectorGridCenterLdu`, rather than being handled by a per-connector seat plane alone. The measured Builder frames differ per part in both turn and origin, so this is needed for the general case and not only for `93273`. (Owner decision, 2026-08-05.)
- Licence must not block this work, because it is private and noncommercial: a CC BY-SA source such as the LDCad shadow library may be admitted as catalog truth, and a share-alike question is not a reason to leave a part unbuildable. What that does not waive, and what every such admission still carries: per-source attribution travelling with the derived data and rendered into `docs/bundled-geometry-notices.md` from the catalog itself, an honest record in `docs/dependency-data-bom.md` that share-alike would attach to the derived data on redistribution, and training rights that stay unheld. Record the caveat rather than quietly dropping it, and never commit a file from the share-alike source — derived positions, not source text. (Owner decision, 2026-08-05, which unblocked `30357`: absent from the Builder pack entirely, LDraw alone gave it 8 studs and zero clutches, and the real booklet build stopped at step 16 for want of exactly that.)
