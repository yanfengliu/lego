# Part model and catalog truth

Status date: 2026-09-24

This document owns how a part is identified, represented, sourced, derived, checked, admitted, and versioned. [`spec.md`](spec.md) owns document and validator semantics, [`building-system.md`](building-system.md) owns the product frontier, and the [dependency and data bill of materials](../dependency-data-bom.md) owns source, license, and allowed-role records.

## Current catalog

The builtin catalog is `builtin.basic-parts/32` with 106 definitions. Sixty-one render from repository-authored parametric recipes and 45 render bundled, content-hashed LDraw surfaces with source-faithful normals. Sixteen of the mesh-backed definitions are render-only promotions of earlier parametric parts; the other twenty-nine are fully measured definitions. Seventy-seven definitions retain repository-authored physical recipes. Every parametric definition carries the LDraw-to-catalog frame (an orientation and a whole-LDU translation) measured from its official LDraw file by `scripts/derive-ldraw-catalog-frames.mjs` into `ldraw-interchange-frames.generated.ts`; a mesh-backed definition's frame is its `assetToCatalogFrame`.

Current connection-semantics truth and overall truth are both `sha256:dbb4c8147a20b8ed0598ebf617583993fa7dd89788cdc491056d1579ee7b19d6`: catalog `builtin.basic-parts/32` at `sha256:2d95bc0b371954519d2f04bc56a950036c63de162469688d62cba9bee4fe2832`, connector taxonomy `stud-tube/2` at `sha256:40354eb03e33724c019a25b0109c971816a3c6cab20d85d7edbba8df33e25de1`, collision model `rectilinear-stud-clearance/4` at `sha256:47790408ba997723a9f535cf0d3ca06a520ec6cc6eb97a18fe4b069638a3e472`, transform policy `part-scoped-proper-orientations-negative-y-up/1` at `sha256:44cf428cee1487a9441c609a75fbafefd6c3b4591512af30f8903e4508285f4c`, and validator set `lego.kernel-validators/5` at `sha256:44233e884c474210006e4e94b82e952fd7b446768396d5b53575eb7946cba4fe`.

The fail-closed historical connection-semantic registry reconstructs 33 source truths and 2,342 current endpoints at `sha256:1f6a6fccc50ccf2b2688ddc61e078717b8230db3d7c8478ba9446a427316d7ef`; exhaustive shared-identity review records 970 endpoint deltas and zero reachable-pair deltas, with four current reachable pairs at `sha256:92dd1cdfb9f34879f55a5ee5a0827b5c24c830da654c90bd3b00896025ca5731`. Current and historical evidence artifacts are separately pinned and parsed, so a missing, stale, or relabeled artifact refuses rather than silently becoming current truth.

Source-to-catalog, saved-document, and LDraw interchange frames share a generated vocabulary of exactly 24 determinant-`+1` signed-permutation rotations, ordered at roster `sha256:57446894cd2b917eb5463672655baa012d7c03539ba4147cd89e9c774a309201` and reproduced byte-for-byte in TypeScript and Python. Catalog legality is part-scoped rather than globally upright: every current part retains the four upright yaws, and eleven definitions opt into specific evidence-backed non-upright additions from the same 24-row vocabulary. The scorer derives each inverse from the complete registry, LDraw import requires the corrected document transform to resolve to one of the selected part's legal orientations, and reflections are rejected. A catalog version change by itself never authorizes a printed placement or widens another part's legal orientations.

Every part has one LDraw-to-catalog frame, catalog = O * ldraw + t, with t the catalog point the LDraw origin lands on. A mesh part's is its `assetToCatalogFrame`. Since `/30` every parametric part declares its own as `ldrawFrame` (orientation, whole-LDU translation and per-file provenance), measured by `scripts/derive-ldraw-catalog-frames.mjs` from the byte-pinned official LDraw archive into `packages/catalog/src/ldraw-interchange-frames.generated.ts`: of the four upright yaws, a candidate must put the file's full extent exactly on the catalog bounds and every catalog stud on a stud primitive, and candidates the file's own faces cannot tell apart are one placement. 59 of the 61 rows derive; `32062` and `3483` are a reviewed choice among measured candidates. A 2 x 4 plate is `upright-yaw-90` with `[0, -4, 0]`. Before `/30` a parametric part had a zero offset and, except the 2 x 14 plate, no turn, so LDraw export missed at least its top-face offset (4 LDU for a plate, 12 for a brick) and often a quarter turn; LDraw subset `/2` files use the measured frames. `/1` files are read through the frames of `/29`, the ones real `/1` files from commit `982634d` pin (`ldraw-subset-v1-fixtures.test.ts`); a `/1` file written before a part's mesh promotion (`/12`, `/13`) is read through today's mesh frame instead of the one it was written with. Under `LEGO_RUN_EVIDENCE=1`, `--check` and `scripts/ldraw-catalog-frame-derivation.test.mjs` re-derive the table from the archive byte for byte; a clean clone lacks the archive, so without the opt-in both say they did not run.

Catalog `/32` adds no part identity. It gives `41682` the two clutch seats its exact LDCad root authors in the back recess of its upright wall, a square-S6 socket where booklet step 40 presses a 1 × 2 round-end plate's studs. They face +Z and are appended as `undersideClutch:4` and `:5`, so the four underside seats keep their ids and a saved `/31` edge on any of the part's existing ports migrates unchanged. The part's collision is measured as solid intervals per column instead of a height field, which leaves the recess empty (see [its row](#measured-mesh-definitions)).

Catalog `/31` adds no part identity either: it gives the studs of seven measured parts (`2450`, `79491`, `28802`, `15254`, `41682`, `2877`, `32064`) the `nominal-stud-tube/1` connection profile the other 14 source-radius parts already had, so a validated edge can seat a clutch on them; ordinary collision keeps the measured radius. Catalog `/30` adds no part identity. Besides the measured frames, it gives the `15573` jumper plate a centre underside seat, `undersideClutch:center`. It is an alternate clutch seat half a pitch off the stud grid, admitted by the one rule in `packages/catalog/src/alternate-clutch-seats.ts` that also admits the measured `99563` and `3245c` seats. The seat shares capacity with the two grid clutches either side of it: it claims both cells, each grid clutch claims one, and a connection consumes every cell its endpoints name.

The sixteen render-only promotions take their visible triangles, normals, exact bounds, and explicit source-to-catalog frame from LDraw while preserving their preceding connector, allowance, connector-grid, evidence, and collision declarations. Their `preserved-catalog-recipe` collision can fill a visible void and is not proof of hollow physical truth. The `/13` migration therefore reports changed render interpretation without pretending that physical semantics changed.

Booklet coverage is measured by `npm run booklet` against LEGO's official model, not by counting catalog rows. A design is covered `exact` when a catalog part declares its exact LDraw file and carries connectors and collision, and `interchangeable` when a part declares another mould revision that the catalog's variant policy calls interchangeable; the two are counted apart. At `/30` the catalog covers 82 of the booklet's 172 designs (77 exact, 5 interchangeable) and misses 90, the first needed at step 51. The current position lives in [`building-system.md`](building-system.md).

Mould-revision suffixes are exact where they change construction. `isInterchangeableLdrawMouldRevisionBase` in `packages/catalog/src/ldraw-variant-policy.ts` treats most trailing-letter revisions as interchangeable, but `2453` and `3245` name physically distinct variants. The catalog admits exact `2453b`, `3245b`, and `3245c`; no caller may add or remove a suffix to manufacture an identity the source did not publish. A suffix-absent Builder record such as `3245;M` or `2453;I` is therefore never bound to a suffixed row by inference, and the `3245` Builder diagnostic stays unresolved.

Identity evidence changes no catalog truth. A callout crop, a closed-set identification, a model reply, or a frame registry may name an element or design, but it grants no catalog definition or alias, geometry, connector, collision, frame, physical assignment, placement, or completion authority, and model output stays untrusted. The retired first-50 campaign's identity, closure, and frame artifacts are history: see [`docs/devlog/summary.md`](../devlog/summary.md) and branch `archive/first50-campaign-wip-20260908` (`f44f1b8`). Its reviewed `/29` frame registry, `output/real-build/history/prefix50-ldraw-catalog-frames-reviewed-move-bcf9702150b73cab1bd70d7ecd0bf33b3b3917522ce4f0ca892be56424b861a1.json`, is ignored run evidence; `npm run booklet` compares against it only under `LEGO_RUN_EVIDENCE=1`.

`npm run parts:check` covers all 106 definitions and is part of `npm run verify`. It is a declaration-consistency gate, not a substitute for source review, matched visual inspection, connector evidence, collision review, or browser verification.

Each mesh-backed admission from `/13` through `/29` closed with the eight-view source-versus-catalog review under the truth it was captured in: every original 640 × 640 pair was inspected at native size and recorded `same`. Those packets and review sidecars were ignored run evidence under `output/part-visual-admission/` and are not retained; the devlog records the tranches. A review is bound to its capture truth, so after a truth change it is historical evidence and is never relabeled as current (see the [part visual-admission runbook](../runbooks/part-visual-admission.md)). Matched exterior pixels never establish hidden interiors, connector or collision truth, physical frame, grip, stability, Builder-revision binding, placement, or completion authority.

## One part, four representations

Each representation answers a different question. None is authority for another.

### 1. Render surface

The viewport and palette preview resolve the same catalog definition. Parametric parts derive visible geometry from bounded features; mesh-backed parts use preloaded LDraw triangles, source-derived normals, a pinned content hash, and one explicit asset-to-catalog frame.

Render geometry is disposable view data. It cannot authorize placement, connection, collision, catalog admission, or a claim about hidden physical material. “Exact source geometry” in this repository means the admitted LDraw triangles, loader-faithful normals, and pinned source-to-catalog frame, not pixel identity across independent render paths or access to LEGO production CAD.

### 2. Construction lattice and bounds

Placement uses LDraw units and legal catalog orientations. Footprints, nominal heights, exact body bounds where needed, and connector centres provide the lattice used for enumeration and pruning.

Exact decimal bounds are retained when a measured extent cannot be projected safely as an ordinary binary float. The catalog derives the float projection from that declaration and refuses inward rounding. Off-lattice articulated poses retain their rigid transform and use geometric validators rather than becoming lattice truth.

### 3. Connection field

Catalog ports carry local transforms, capacity, gender, profile, and one of eleven implemented kinds: `stud`, `undersideClutch`, `axle`, `axleHole`, `blindAxleHole`, `pin`, `pinHole`, `bar`, `clip`, `hinge`, and `hingeSocket`. Seven pair rules cover stud/clutch, axle/axle-hole, axle/blind-axle-hole, axle/pin-hole, pin/pin-hole, bar/clip, and hinge/hinge-socket behavior.

`undersideClutch` names the female stud-tube seat, not its direction. A clutch's outward normal is one signed coordinate axis, and its orientation names that axis (`connector-down` for +Y, `connector-z-positive` for +Z). Its tube-seat allowance is the nominal stud volume, centred half the stud height inside the seat along -normal; a validated edge relieves host solid only inside it, along whichever axis. A measured blueprint writes a seat facing +Y as `[x, y, z]` and any other as `[x, y, z, nx, ny, nz]`, carried from the LDCad route through the part plan and the emitter. Admission checks the seat plane and the stud footprint along that normal, and a part's new seats are appended so its existing ids keep their numbers. At `/32` the only seats not facing +Y are `41682`'s two recess seats.

Placement discovery consumes those rules, but document edges and attach programs still serialize only `stud-tube`; referenced ports imply the actual pair. A versioned pair identity is still needed before the full taxonomy is represented end to end.

A visible cylinder or cavity does not prove a functional connector. Builder or LDCad fields may supply that claim only through a pinned source and frame, with insertion volume and backing checked against independent geometry.

### 4. Collision and physics

Collision is a bounded union of simple primitives for deterministic validation and compound rigid bodies. It must contain represented outer material while leaving functional connector paths open.

Parametric features derive collision where that derivation is justified. Twenty-one fully measured mesh definitions use conservative column height fields, which fill each 1-LDU column over the whole span of surface it meets, so a cavity opening sideways is filled. Since `/32`, `41682` uses per-column solid intervals instead (`scripts/part_admission_solid_columns.py`, opted into per part plan): BFC crossings along each column keep only the intervals inside the surface, a slab a face passes through stays solid, an open underside tube keeps its full height, and a column whose surface is not closed is refused. Sixteen render-only promotions preserve their earlier conservative recipes. No mode proves that every visible cavity is physically usable. Compared column by column at `/32`, the height field over-fills seven other measured parts (most in `32064`, `73230` and `3040`) and ends past the measured solid in four (`80015`, `73230`, `35787`, `30503`), while 16 of the 33 part plans have columns the solid-interval derivation refuses; those are findings, not yet changes.

Physics derives rigid components and joints from the connection graph, then builds compound bodies and geometric mass properties from collision. Physical mass remains unknown in current inventory metadata; repeated source defaults are not admitted as truth.

## Declaration and derivation

Repository-authored blueprints produce render recipes, nominal bounds, connector fields, collision primitives, legal orientations, search metadata, and part-standard facts from one bounded declaration. A feature may emit only the representations it can justify; missing geometry is a failing standard, not an invitation for a silent exception.

External meshes deliberately split authority. The generated full-measured path may carry reviewed render, connector, and collision evidence. The render-only path can carry only source closure, visible geometry, normals, bounds, frame, stud witnesses, and attribution; the catalog factory overlays those render fields onto the preceding physical definition and proves the preserved fields unchanged.

The bounded generator verifies archive hashes, path and expansion limits, BFC winding, LDrawLoader-equivalent hard edges, frames, bounds, visible stud witnesses, provenance, and reproducible generated bytes. Runtime admission verifies hashes, indices, normals, finite bounds, represented body collision, and resource ceilings. These checks produce evidence; they do not replace reviewed admission.

Collision containment is a finite barycentric-grid measurement, not a proof over the continuous surface. The requested spacing is validated as finite and positive, each triangle is limited to 256 subdivisions, and current emission reports distinguish the legacy requested-spacing alias from the maximum effective grid spacing and number of cap-limited triangles; zero outside means zero only over that disclosed sample population. The `/23` `32064` row requests 0.25 LDU, reaches a maximum effective spacing of 0.25 LDU without hitting the cap, and samples 561,932 points, while eight older rows disclose their coarser cap-limited resolution instead of presenting the request as achieved.

Collision-field generation discards zero-volume boundary-clamped cells before scoring or emission; a synthetic regression gates that failure class so a surface-only boundary sample cannot masquerade as a positive collision body.

## Semantic part standard

A part must draw what it claims.

- A declared stud is visible at its declared centre and exact radius.
- A declared underside clutch has a visible entrance, cavity, walls, and grip geometry from below.
- Collision neither omits represented outer material nor silently seals a claimed connector path.
- Visual bounds contain the render without inward rounding; collision is checked under its declared mesh-derived or preserved-recipe mode.
- Connector backing, reachability, capacity, and compatibility are independently checked.
- Chiral identity, legal orientation, and source frame agree with the visible hand.
- Palette preview, viewport geometry, catalog metadata, and retained admission images resolve the same definition.

`scripts/check-part-standard.mjs` automates seven current declaration rules: underside geometry, stud radius, declared geometry mode, declared collision, hollow clutch-bearing bodies, body containment, and stud extent. Connector reachability and compatibility, chirality, source frames, render/collision parity, palette parity, and matched visual inspection remain separate test and review obligations.

## Identity, versioning, and sources

A `PartDefinition` carries a namespaced catalog ID, LDraw design identifier and aliases, family and searchable name, geometry recipe and content hash, bounds, collision primitives, connectors, legal orientations, colors, substitution grouping, inventory metadata with physical mass currently unset, and source/license/attribution provenance.

Catalog IDs are semantic identities, not filenames. A changed shape, connector field, collision model, hand, or source frame is changed truth even when its display name stays similar.

Every catalog-truth change advances `BUILTIN_CATALOG_VERSION`, retains supported prior snapshots in `MIGRATABLE_CATALOG_VERSIONS`, and emits an explicit migration report. Each reviewed historical full-truth hash binds its exact immutable catalog-part/color roster and a source-commit-derived connector projection covering port frames, shared capacity cells, compatibility, effective pair rules, connection-authorized allowances, matching stud collision primitives, and any validated stud profile. Document constraints may be a legitimate subset, but a future identifier in either those caller-owned constraints or an actual part blocks migration instead of becoming current truth. A connection on a later or removed endpoint blocks. A connection on a semantically changed endpoint also blocks, unless that exact change belongs to a reviewed carry class. Stable endpoints and unrelated draft-invalid transforms carry forward. Each refusal names the edge and what satisfies it: remove the edge, then re-attach after migration.

Two carry classes exist. The first, `capacity-cells-added-shared-only-with-endpoints-absent-from-source` in `packages/brick-kernel/src/historical-connection-carry-forward.ts`. ## Admission checklist

The reproducible admission workflow is documented in the [part visual-admission runbook](../runbooks/part-visual-admission.md). It keeps the editor's seven-view presentation hook separate from the clean eight-view source-versus-catalog review packet.

Before a new or modified part enters catalog truth:

1. Settle stable identity, family, design ID, hand, aliases, and search name.
2. Record every source closure, revision, hash, license, attribution, and allowed role.
3. Prove one catalog frame aligns visible geometry, bounds, connectors, collision, and legal orientations.
4. Pass the automated part standard and matched top, bottom, front, back, left, right, isometric, and underside-oblique review; add interior or cutaway views where the exterior cannot expose a relevant cavity.
5. Establish connector claims from an authored or repository-owned basis and test insertion, backing, capacity, and compatibility.
6. Review collision separately for represented material, functional voids, and resource limits.
7. Verify palette search/preview and production viewport resolve the admitted geometry.
8. Update catalog migration, schema fixtures, notices, bill of materials, and durable review conclusions.
9. Run focused catalog, renderer, migration, generator, and browser checks followed by `npm run verify`.

Packet generation always leaves review outcomes pending. A separate immutable sidecar binds the packet and both image hashes for each ordered view; only an explicit `same`, `different`, or `not-observable` review closes that evidence. Tests and image metrics support this decision but cannot make it automatically. The completed `/13` sidecars follow this boundary; their all-`same` result is explicitly limited to surfaces visible in the eight retained views.

Missing parts remain work items. A booklet step is never completed by substituting a similar design, dropping a piece, mirroring a hand, or weakening a hard check.
